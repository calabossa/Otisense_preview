The Challenge

  • AOM affects 80-90% of children experiencing at least one episode before age 4,
  • and is a leading cause of paediatric visits worldwide.
  • 30–40% of children will have recurrent AOM episodes.
  • Diagnostic depends only on clinician expertise, and is extremely low.
  • In the U.S., >80% of AOM visits result in antibiotics,
  • although ~70% resolve without them.
  • This overuse drives antimicrobial resistance (AMR).

Our Solution

  • Otisense Medical Device has developed a home-use diagnostic device powered by AI and signal processing.
  • Our devise uses the elereical resoibse of the brain to taste bud stimulation for precise diagnosis.
